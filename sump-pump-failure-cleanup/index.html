Sump Pump Failure Cleanup in Syracuse: What to Know

The moment a basement most needs its sump pump is often the moment the pump gives out. During heavy rain the water table climbs and fills the pit, at which point an outage, a stuck float or an overwhelmed pump stops the pumping. The pit overflows and the basement floor starts to flood. Until pumping resumes, the water continues to rise.

Do not think of it as clean water. It has come through soil and often carries silt, bacteria and whatever was in the drain tile, so we treat it as Category 2 at best and Category 3 if it has sat or mixed with sewage. For the job, this means soaked porous items such as drywall and carpet pad are usually discarded, and what stays gets washed and disinfected first, then dried.

Before We Arrive

  1. Keep Out of the BasementDo not step into a flooded basement while the power is on. The furnace, wall outlets or the cord of the pump itself could be in contact with it.
  2. Shut Off Basement PowerTurn off the basement circuits at the breaker panel, but only if you can reach it from a dry spot. Call the utility when the panel itself sits in the flooded area.
  3. Look at the Pump From a DistanceA tripped outlet, a loose plug or a float wedged against the basin wall are the things to look for. Keep your hands out of the water.
  4. Assume the Water Is DirtyIf going down cannot be avoided, put on gloves and rubber boots, leave pets and children upstairs, and wash up after handling wet items.
  5. Move Valuables and CallTake pictures of the pump and the water level, bring valuables that are still dry upstairs, and phone us. Do not wait for the water to drain away by itself.
What We Do

Our Sump Pump Failure Cleanup Process

1

Get the Water Out

Because the pumps and extraction units are ours, the broken sump does not hold up the work. We clear silt and standing water off the floor, out of low areas and from around the pit.

2

Take Out What Got Wet

Wearing protective gear, we take out the carpet, pad, wet drywall and insulation that the groundwater touched, bag it and haul it away so clean framing and concrete are exposed.

3

Wash and Disinfect

The slab, wall framing and remaining contents are washed down and treated with an antimicrobial product. Drying comes afterward, because fans on a dirty basement would blow contaminants everywhere.

4

Dry and Verify

Dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture meter readings on the framing and concrete return to normal, typically 3 to 5 days. A working pump should be back in the pit before our equipment leaves.

Will Insurance Pay?

Insurance and Sump Pump Failure Cleanup

Sump pump losses are widely misunderstood when it comes to insurance. Standard homeowners coverage generally leaves out sump overflow and backup, while flood coverage is bought separately and follows other rules. Unless you bought an endorsement for sump overflow or water backup there is often no coverage, and such endorsements are capped. Look for that endorsement on your declarations page or ask your agent. We record the pump failure, the water level and the damage so your claim file is complete.

The Usual Culprits

What Causes It

  • The pump being unplugged by accident or sitting on an outlet that tripped.
  • A power outage during the storm with no battery backup on the pump.
  • More water flowing into the pit than an undersized pump can move.
  • An intake screen packed with mud and stones so the pump cannot draw water.
  • Water returning down the discharge pipe because the check valve no longer seals.
What Is at Risk

What the Water Damages

Basement Floor Coverings

Carpet and pad soaked by groundwater cannot be cleaned to a safe standard and are removed. Laminate and vinyl plank trap silty water underneath and usually come up too.

Low Electrical Outlets

Wall outlets near the floor are sometimes submerged in a sump flood. Have an electrician look at wet receptacles and cable before power is restored to them.

Wall Framing

Bottom plates rest directly on concrete, so they are the first framing to get wet. With the wall opened up, this wood is scrubbed, treated and then dried to normal readings.

Drywall and Wall Insulation

Drywall and insulation wick dirty water upward from the slab, well above the visible water line. Because the water is contaminated, the wet lower section is cut out, not dried.
