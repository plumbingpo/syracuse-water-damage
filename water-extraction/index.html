Every water damage restoration job starts with extraction, and no other early step matters more. Structural materials, floors, possessions and building systems all take less damage when standing water comes out sooner. Every hour that water sits on surfaces, it penetrates deeper into materials, soaking through carpet into padding, wicking up drywall, saturating wood framing and seeping into subflooring. For emergency water extraction in Syracuse, Syracuse Restoration brings truck-mounted units and portable extractors built to pull out hundreds of gallons every minute.

Our truck-mounted extraction units are among the most powerful water removal systems available, with the suction to pull water from carpets, hard floors and structural materials far more effectively than consumer or rental equipment. Where the truck hoses will not reach, we bring in submersible pumps, strong portable extractors and specialty tools made for water caught between floor layers, inside wall cavities or in tight spaces.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Using moisture detection equipment, our IICRC-certified team finds water that has moved out of sight, whether under cabinets, beneath flooring, behind baseboards or inside walls. The more moisture our specialty tools take from these concealed areas ahead of drying, the shorter and lighter the structural drying job becomes, and the lower the chance of secondary mold growth.

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Add our number, (631) 535-3026, to your cell so help is one tap away in a water emergency
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and label it clearly
  • Critical areas of the house should each have a leak sensor that you know is working
  • Early leak signs show up near appliances, toilets and sink cabinets, so check those spots on a schedule
  • A sump pump that has not been checked may fail in a storm, so test it ahead of wet weather
  • Keep valuables and stored boxes off the basement floor on shelves or pallets

The Benefits of Professional Water Extraction

The speed and thoroughness of water extraction directly determine the extent of damage to your property and the cost of restoration. Truck-mounted extractors move water at hundreds of times the rate of consumer tools, and a job that could cost you a full day with a shop vac is done by our machines in minutes. That gap in speed is more than a convenience, because each hour water touches building materials can add thousands of dollars in further damage.

Hidden moisture, the kind consumer tools never reach, is part of a professional extraction too. It follows plumbing lines and electrical conduits, slips between flooring layers, climbs drywall by wicking and is drawn into carpet pad through capillary action. Our specialty extraction tools and techniques target these hidden moisture deposits, removing significantly more water than surface extraction alone. This thorough approach reduces drying time, lowers equipment costs and, most importantly, prevents the hidden mold growth that results from trapped moisture.

How to Tell You Need Water Extraction

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Visible water where your furnace, water heater or crawl space access is
  • Stairwells or window wells that fill up and spill water inside
  • Rising water in the basement because the sump pump stopped working
  • Water sitting on a floor anywhere in the building, no matter where it came from
  • Water accumulating after a fire suppression system discharges
  • Drips or a steady stream coming out of a ceiling, a wall or a fixture
  • Water squeezing up between floorboards, planks or tiles when you walk
