A burst pipe is one of the most common water damage events a property can face, and potentially one of the most destructive. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

Day or night, Syracuse Restoration responds to burst pipe emergencies across Syracuse and the nearby communities. We can help find the shutoff and stop the water, and our team works with licensed plumbers to see that the pipe itself is repaired correctly. With the source under control, our crew goes straight to extraction, using truck-mounted machines and portable extractors to clear standing water from each affected area quickly.

After the water is out, we dry every material that took on moisture: finished floors, subflooring, wood framing, insulation and drywall. Since water runs downhill through ceiling spaces, floor assemblies and wall cavities, a burst pipe frequently affects several rooms on more than one level. Our thermal imaging and moisture detection equipment traces water migration paths so every affected area is identified and properly dried, which prevents the mold growth and structural deterioration that hidden moisture causes.

Burst Pipe Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Leave the heat on when you travel in winter so indoor pipes stay above freezing
  • Disconnect garden hoses and shut off outdoor faucets before the first freeze
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and how to operate it quickly
  • Aging galvanized or polybutylene plumbing should be swapped out ahead of an unexpected failure
  • Have a licensed plumber inspect your plumbing system every few years for wear and corrosion
  • Wrap any bare pipe that runs through a crawl space, garage or attic without heat

The Benefits of Professional Burst Pipe Repair

Burst pipe water damage often extends far beyond what is visible on the surface. Water travels through wall cavities, along electrical conduits, through floor assemblies and between ceiling joists, saturating materials in places you may not discover for days or weeks. At that point mold can already be growing out of sight, and the structure may have lost a significant amount of strength. With moisture meters and thermal cameras, trained technicians follow the water's path and pinpoint every affected area right away.

The machines we use for extraction and drying are in another class from anything sold for home use. Compared with a shop vacuum, truck-mounted extraction is hundreds of times faster, and compared with a residential dehumidifier, a commercial one removes 10 to 20 times as much moisture. Mold can begin growing in 24 to 48 hours, so this kind of power and speed matters a great deal.

How to Tell You Need Burst Pipe Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Pipes coated in ice or frost when temperatures drop
  • Brownish water at the faucet, which can mean corroded pipes
  • Damp, moldy odors around sinks, tubs or walls that hide pipes
  • A spray or steady drip coming off pipework you can see
  • Puddles on the floor that you cannot trace to anything obvious
  • A fixture that stops flowing when it is below freezing outside
  • Ceilings or walls that show damp patches, stains or a swollen bulge
