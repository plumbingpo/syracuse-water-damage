How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Being able to find the electrical panel quickly means wet areas can be de-energized without risk.
  • Shut off the water at the main whenever you leave home for more than a few days.
  • Put leak alarms beneath sinks and beside the dishwasher, washer and water heater.
  • Locate the main shutoff valve now, and show each person at home how to turn it off.
  • Aging supply lines to toilets, faucets and washing machines should be swapped out before one fails.
  • Save an emergency restoration company's number in your contacts ahead of time.

Emergency Water Damage Restoration: What Homeowners Ask

If water has already soaked floors, walls or ceilings, call a restoration team first. On arrival our crew isolates or closes the supply, then starts extraction immediately. A plumber is still needed for the permanent pipe repair, which can usually happen during drying. Standing water does more damage each hour, so do not let it sit until the repair.
If the main valve is safe to reach, close it. At the panel, switch off circuits serving wet areas, and stay out of standing water while doing so. Get electronics, area rugs and smaller valuables into a dry room. Take photos for your insurer. Keep pets and children clear, and never wade into possible sewage.
That comes down to cause and policy terms. Sudden and accidental damage, such as a burst pipe, is often covered. Flood water from outside and long-running leaks typically are not, absent separate flood insurance. Because most policies expect prompt action to limit the loss, stop the water without waiting for an adjuster. Your agent or policy has the answer.
Drying usually takes 3 to 5 days once equipment is running. During that period a technician rechecks moisture levels and takes out units that are no longer needed. Bigger jobs and dense materials like plaster or hardwood may need extra time. Leave the machines on even though they are loud and warm, because drying stalls without them.
Cost depends mainly on how much area is wet, the category of water and how much equipment the job needs. Some after-hours calls carry an added charge, and we will tell you about any before we send a crew. Quick action usually brings the overall bill down, since fewer materials are ruined and drying is shorter.

Signs You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Syracuse

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.
  • The house was flooded when you walked in, with no telling how many hours it had been going.
  • An outlet, a light fixture or the ceiling above has water dripping out of it.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.
  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.
  • Water has reached the electrical panel, appliances or power strips on the floor.
