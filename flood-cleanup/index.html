Floods start in many ways: heavy rain, storm surge, a river over its banks, a dead sump pump, a sewer backup or a major plumbing failure, and all of them call for fast professional help. In nearly every case the water falls into Category 2, known as gray water, or Category 3, known as black water, which means contaminants, bacteria and other hazards that are a real health risk. Day or night, 24/7, Syracuse Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout Syracuse to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. Next our technicians, who hold IICRC certification, judge the level of contamination and choose a sanitizing plan that covers HEPA air filtration, antimicrobial application and disinfecting each surface that got wet. Anything unsalvageable comes out and is hauled off correctly, including wet insulation, drywall that took in contaminated water and waterlogged carpet padding.

Once the space is extracted and sanitized, our team brings in commercial drying gear, namely low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers plus high-velocity air movers, so the structural components dry all the way through. Daily readings from calibrated moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera tell us when everything is fully dry, which is what keeps mold from growing. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

Why Flood Cleanup Is Not a DIY Job

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. Our technicians rely on psychrometric calculations, meter readings and thermal imaging to verify that each affected spot, from the framing to the subfloor to the inside of the walls, meets accepted drying standards. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.

Flood Cleanup Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Roof runoff should leave the downspout far enough out that it cannot soak the foundation
  • Clear leaves and debris from storm drains and gutters, which lets water drain the way it should
  • Keep documents and keepsakes in watertight bins on shelves, not on a basement floor
  • Consider flood insurance even if you are not in a designated flood zone
  • Make sure the ground slopes away from your foundation on every side
  • Coat basement walls with waterproofing and seal any cracks in the foundation

Signs You Need Flood Cleanup in Syracuse

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water coming in through foundation cracks, window wells or door thresholds
  • Heavy rainfall, a dead sump pump and water spreading across the basement
  • Tide marks on drywall or trim where the water peaked
  • Dirty water rising out of floor drains or sewer lines into rooms you use
  • Appliances such as a water heater, washer or dishwasher spilling water across the floor
  • Standing water anywhere on your property, whatever the source
  • A layer of silt, dirt or debris on the floor after water recedes
